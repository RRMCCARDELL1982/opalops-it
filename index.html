What we do

Technology that works, from doors-open to teardown.

Event technology fails at the worst possible moment — during badge printing at a packed registration line, mid-setup with a network that won't hold. OpalOps IT is built to prevent that: we plan the technical footprint before your event, deploy it correctly, and stay on-site to keep it running.

We work directly with event producers, venues, and corporate clients to make sure registration systems, networks, and on-site devices perform reliably before, during, and after the event.

"The tech should be invisible. You only notice it when it breaks — so we make sure it doesn't."
Our services

Event technology, covered end to end.

Four core service areas — scoped to the technical footprint of your event, not the guest experience around it.

▧

Pre-Event Technology Planning

We work with your team ahead of time to map out technical requirements — device counts, network needs, registration flow, and power/layout logistics — so the site is ready to go before doors open. Includes a walkthrough or floor-plan review and a punch list for day-of setup.

▤

Workstation & Registration Setup

On-site build-out of check-in stations, kiosks, badge printers, and staff workstations — cabling, power distribution, device staging, and layout execution to your registration vendor's specs.

◈

Network / Wi-Fi Deployment

Setup and configuration of temporary event networks — access points, switches, and guest/staff SSIDs — sized to attendee load and venue constraints, with signal testing before go-live.

▣

Printer & Device Support

On-site setup, troubleshooting, and consumables management (labels, ribbons, paper) for badge printers, ticket scanners, tablets, and check-in hardware throughout the event.

↻

On-Site Troubleshooting

Dedicated tech support staff stationed on the floor for the event's duration to resolve connectivity, hardware, or registration-flow issues in real time — minimizing lines and downtime.
